About

I am an economist with 14+ years at the Central Reserve Bank of Peru, where I forecast inflation and build nowcasting and machine learning models from high-frequency data. My work combines applied econometrics, time series and machine learning with reproducible data pipelines: microdata panels, automated data collection, mixed-frequency models and formal out-of-sample evaluation.

I hold an MSc in Economics and Finance from the Barcelona School of Economics and an MSc in Applied Mathematics from PUCP, and I teach Machine Learning in the MSc in Artificial Intelligence at UPC.
