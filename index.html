About

I am an economist with 14+ years at the Central Reserve Bank of Peru, where I forecast inflation and build nowcasting and machine learning models from high-frequency data. My work combines applied econometrics, time series and machine learning with reproducible data pipelines: microdata panels, automated data collection, mixed-frequency models and formal out-of-sample evaluation.

My research uses high-frequency data to answer macro questions, such as real-time forecasting aligned with the release calendar and credit conditions. I evaluate models out of sample, in real time, and report what fails as well as what works.

I hold an MSc in Economics and Finance from the Barcelona School of Economics and an MSc in Applied Mathematics from PUCP, and I teach Machine Learning in the MSc in Artificial Intelligence at UPC.

Projects

  • Nowcasting Peruvian inflation with mixed-frequency and machine learning models — U-MIDAS, Almon-MIDAS, Ridge, LASSO, XGBoost and a forecast combination on daily BCRP data, evaluated out of sample (2015-01 to 2026-08) with Diebold-Mariano tests. For headline inflation the combination cuts RMSE 3.5% relative to an AR (p = 0.098), but only 0.9% relative to an AR with inflation expectations; for core inflation no model beats the AR. The honest finding: daily data add little beyond expectations.
  • Lima wholesale food prices: daily pipeline and dashboard — automated download of MIDAGRI-SISAP wholesale prices since 2010, published data-quality checks (359,680 clean observations, 79 isolated source glitches removed), price-change, volatility, shock and price-pressure indicators, and a live dashboard rebuilt every day.
  • Credit risk: PD scorecard and second-line validation — WoE logistic scorecard, XGBoost challenger, LGD and lifetime expected loss on 36-month Lending Club loans, with a validation report. Out-of-time AUC 0.639 (Lending Club grade: 0.661); validation finds PD under-predicts default out of time (calibration ratio 0.890) and recommends recalibration.
  • Bank account opening fraud: decisions, fairness and drift — XGBoost vs logistic regression on 1M Feedzai BAF applications with a strictly time-based design (tuned on months 0-4, calibrated and thresholded on month 5, evaluated once on months 6-7). Detects 58% of fraud with a threshold set for 5% false positives (6.2% realised as fraud rose), audits false positives by age (2.3x higher for applicants aged 50+) and ends in a rule-based recommendation to a risk committee, documented in a model card.
